# Cutting Words at the table — a test handoff (2026-10-14)

For a sit-down on the sandbox, to walk what By the Books does with Cutting Words since the 2026-10-14 build (RULINGS *The
asks widened*). Written for people at a table, not for a console: every step is a thing a player or the DM does in the game,
and what each seat should see. Nothing here needs a typed command. The sandbox runs the working copy; prod is untouched.

**Who.** Robert plays **Cecil Edmunds**, a College of Lore Bard 5 on the sandbox since today: Cutting Words, four Bardic
Inspiration dice (d8), back on a Short Rest. Drew plays **Kartor Bloodaxe**. Tom plays **Tychon Thorne** (his Portent is the
other handoff). Matt is the DM. The foe is the **Hearth-guard Drill Post** on Party Camp, hostile, with two attacks on its
token: **Drill Strike** (+4 to hit, 1d8) for the close calls and **Heavy Swing** (+6 to hit, 3d10) for the big ones.
Everyone logs in as themselves: Cecil's questions go to Robert's screen while he is connected, and to the DM's only when he
is not.

**Two rules that explain most of what you will see.**

- The module asks Cecil only when a d8 can make a difference, and only about a foe's roll against a friend or a friend's
  roll against a foe. **A player character swinging at another player character asks nobody**; that is the same-side rule,
  not a gap.
- Cutting Words has two halves in the book, and they arrive at different moments: the **roll half** right after the attack
  roll shows, while the damage waits; the **damage half** after the damage is rolled, while it waits to land.

## 1. The roll half — a close hit

1. The DM has the Drill Post **Drill Strike** Kartor and the roll **hits by a little** (AC 14 or so; a 15 to 21 total).
2. **Robert sees** a window titled "Cutting Words — Hearth-guard Drill Post's attack at Kartor Bloodaxe", with the verdict
   ("17 vs AC 14 — a hit · a d8 can turn it: 17 − 8 = 9") and the choices as round buttons:
   - Cutting Words −d8
   - **Pass just this time** (ticked by default)
   - Don't ask again till next turn
   - Don't ask again this combat

   and one button, **Apply**. A bar drains for the Decision Timer's seconds. Nothing lands on Kartor meanwhile.
3. Robert ticks **Cutting Words −d8** and presses Apply. The d8 lands in chat as Cecil's roll. If it is enough, the attack
   card says "Cutting Words (Cecil Edmunds) −5 — 17 → 12, MISS" and no damage rolls. If the d8 came up short, the hit stands
   and the damage rolls; Cecil's die and Reaction are spent either way.
4. A hit **by more than 8** asks Robert nothing at the roll. Go to section 2.
5. Press Enter instead of ticking anything: that is Pass. The hit stands.

Write down: whose screen the window opened on, and whether the bar ran out before Robert pressed.

## 2. The damage half — every hit the d8 cannot turn

1. The DM has the Drill Post **Heavy Swing** Kartor and it hits well clear of his AC. Nobody is asked at the roll.
2. The damage rolls. Then a card lands: "Kartor Bloodaxe is about to take 17 damage · Cecil Edmunds may cut · from
   Hearth-guard Drill Post", and **Robert sees** "Cutting Words — Hearth-guard Drill Post's hit on Kartor Bloodaxe":
   "hits Kartor Bloodaxe for 17 — cut the damage?", "within 60 ft of Hearth-guard Drill Post · a Reaction · Bardic
   Inspiration 4 left", and the rows:
   - Cut the damage −d8
   - **Pass just this time** (ticked)
   - Don't ask again till next turn
   - Don't ask again this combat
3. Tick **Cut the damage** and Apply. The d8 lands in chat as "Cutting Words — off Kartor Bloodaxe's damage". The receipt on
   the damage card says "reduced by N". Kartor takes that much less. A Bardic Inspiration and Cecil's Reaction are spent.
4. Do it again with a **Pass**: the damage lands whole, nothing spent.
5. **One ask per hit.** If Robert was asked at the roll (section 1) and passed, the damage half does not ask him again for
   that same hit, unless section 4 applies.

## 3. The mutes

1. On any Cutting Words window, tick **Don't ask again till next turn** and Apply. Cecil's effects show "Cutting Words —
   muted until your next turn". The next hit on anyone asks Robert nothing, at the roll or at the damage.
2. Advance the tracker to Cecil's turn. The effect goes. The next hit asks again.
3. Tick **Don't ask again this combat**. The effect reads "muted this combat"; nothing asks until the fight ends, and the
   effect goes with it.
4. **Out of combat** (no tracker running): the window has one way out, **Don't ask again till the next combat**. Press it,
   and Cecil shows "muted until the next combat"; the next swing asks nothing; starting a combat clears it on Cecil's first
   turn.
5. Deleting the effect from the sheet unmutes at once.

## 4. The down guard — the one ask that is never silenced

1. Mute Cecil (section 3, either scope). Set Kartor low: a few Hit Points more than the Drill Post's next hit would deal,
   or simply low enough that a Heavy Swing drops him.
2. The Drill Post hits Kartor. If the damage would take him to 0 and a d8 could keep him up (the damage is at most 8 more
   than his Hit Points plus temporary Hit Points), **Robert is asked anyway**: the window carries **"You can keep Kartor
   Bloodaxe from hitting 0 Hit Points."** and has only Cut and Pass, no mute rows. The hold card says a Reaction can keep
   him up.
3. Cut. Kartor stands, at 1 or a little more. Pass, and he goes down with the usual card.
4. A hit that would drop him by more than a d8 can cover asks nothing while Cecil is muted: the d8 could not save him.

## 5. Cutting Words on a check

1. The DM requests a **Strength (Athletics) check at DC 15** for the Drill Post (an enricher link's *Request Roll*, or
   its sheet with the DC typed in). The Drill Post succeeds by a little.
2. **Robert sees** "Cutting Words — Hearth-guard Drill Post's check", "18 vs DC 15 · a d8 can turn it"; Cut: the card
   reads "Cutting Words (Cecil Edmunds) −5: 18 → 13 vs DC 15 — FAILURE".
3. The Drill Post rolls a **bare** Athletics check from its sheet, no DC. Robert is still asked (a check with no DC always
   can matter), and the card says "ask your DM whether it still succeeds" with the arithmetic. That is the old shape.
4. Kartor's own check, pass or fail, asks nobody: a friend's check is not Cutting Words' to bend.

## 6. The reach and the quiet cases

1. Move Cecil more than 60 ft from the **Drill Post** (the attacker, not Kartor). The Drill Post hits Kartor: nothing asks
   Robert at either half.
2. Kartor swings at Cecil, or Cecil at Kartor: nothing. The same side.
3. Cecil's Reaction already spent this round (he used it on something else): nothing until his next turn.
4. No Bardic Inspiration left: nothing, until a Short Rest.
5. Tychon's Portent and Cecil's Cutting Words on the same hit: each gets its own window; the first to act settles the roll,
   and a hit Portent turned to a miss never reaches Cecil's damage half.

## 7. Write down

The time, who swung at whom, the attack total and the AC, which seat saw the window, which row was ticked, and what the
attack card and the damage card said afterwards. The chat log keeps every die; the module's records sit behind the cards.
