# Portent at the table — a test handoff (2026-10-14)

For a sit-down on the sandbox with the Echoes of Halruaa party, to walk what By the Books does with Portent since the
2026-10-14 build (RULINGS *Portent widened*). Written for people at a table, not for a console: every step is a thing a
player or the DM does in the game, and what each seat should see. Nothing here needs a typed command.

**Who.** Tom plays **Tychon Thorne**, the diviner (on the sandbox his copy holds Portent). Drew plays **Kartor Bloodaxe**.
Anthony plays **Lysandra Vane**. Robert plays **Cecil Edmunds**. Matt is the DM. The foe is the **Hearth-guard Drill Post**
on Party Camp (hostile, so it counts as the other side). Everyone logs in as themselves: the diviner's questions go to Tom's
screen while he is connected, and to Matt's only when he is not.

**One thing to prepare before the session.** A check with a difficulty on it is what the new offer needs, and the cleanest
way to ask for one at the table is dnd5e's **Request Roll**: Matt writes a Dexterity check at DC 15 and a Wisdom save at
DC 15 into the session journal beforehand (dnd5e's check and save links), so at the table he clicks the link's *Request Roll*
and a card lands in chat for the players to click. That is a click, not a command, once it is prepared. A monster's or
trap's own check with a DC works the same way.

## 1. The Long Rest — Tom rolls the dice

1. Tom takes a **Long Rest** on Tychon's sheet.
2. **Tom sees** a window titled "Portent — the Long Rest" with one button, **Roll**, and a draining bar. The rest card in
   chat says "Portent — Tychon Thorne rolls the dice".
3. Tom presses **Roll**. The two d20 tumble as Tychon's own roll, flavoured "Portent — the Long Rest's dice". Under it:
   "Portent — 17 and 3 kept" (whatever the dice say). Tychon's effects show "Portent — 17 · 3".
4. **Matt sees** the same roll in chat and the chip on Tychon. Matt sees no window: it was Tom's to answer.
5. Later, Tom rests again and **closes the window without pressing**. The rest card keeps a **Roll** button. When the
   Decision Timer runs out the dice roll on their own, marked "(timer)".

Write down: whose screen the window opened on, and whether the bar ran out before Tom pressed.

## 2. Tychon's own rolls — before the roll

1. Tom makes an **Arcana check** from the sheet. The roll window carries a box "By the Books — before the roll" with a tick
   per stored face: "Portent — use the 17".
2. Tom ticks a face and rolls. The d20 is that face. The card says "Portent — the d20 is the 17". The chip loses it.
3. The same on a **saving throw** from the sheet and on a **Fire Bolt** attack.
4. If the box is missing on any of these, note whether Shift, Alt or Ctrl was held: those skip the roll window entirely,
   and nothing can be offered without it.

## 3. Someone else's saving throw the module demanded — unchanged

1. Tychon casts **Sleep** or **Grease** at the Drill Post. Matt rolls its save when asked.
2. If a stored face would turn the result the party's way (the foe's success to a failure), **Tom sees** a window
   "Portent — Hearth-guard Drill Post's saving throw" listing only the faces that turn it. The verdict waits for him.
3. Tom answers. The save's card shows the replaced d20 and the new verdict. The chip loses the face.
4. Kartor's **Battleaxe** topples the Drill Post: its Constitution save is demanded the same way, and the same offer stands.

## 4. A check with a difficulty on it — new

1. Matt clicks the prepared **Dexterity check, DC 15** and chooses *Request Roll*. A request card lands in chat.
2. Drew clicks it for Kartor and fails.
3. **Tom sees** "Portent — Kartor Bloodaxe's check", the total against DC 15, and only the faces that turn it. Nothing
   waits on him: Kartor's check already stands as rolled until Tom answers.
4. Tom answers. Kartor's check card gains the line "Portent (Tychon Thorne) the stored 17 replaces the d20 (4): 6 → 19 vs
   DC 15 — SUCCESS". The chip loses the face.

## 5. A requested saving throw — new

1. Matt clicks the prepared **Wisdom save, DC 15**, *Request Roll*. Anthony clicks it for Lysandra and fails.
2. **Tom sees** the same window for Lysandra's saving throw; he answers; the save's own card carries the line with the
   verdict. Nothing waited on him, because no spell demanded this save.

## 6. What stays quiet — on purpose

1. Drew rolls a **Perception check** from Kartor's sheet, no difficulty known. **Nobody is asked.** Tom's faces are not
   offered on a check the DM has not set a DC on.
2. The Drill Post **hits** Kartor on an ordinary roll. Nobody is asked. (Only a natural 20 asks Tom, as before.)
3. Kartor **misses** the Drill Post. Nobody is asked.
4. Tom's own Reaction, uses and slots are untouched by any of this: Portent spends faces, nothing else.

## 8. The asks widened — the next build (RULINGS *The asks widened*, on the sandbox, not in 3.4.6)

The second round of rulings, the same day. Anthony's **Lysandra Vane** needs **Cutting Words** for the first three (a Lore
bard's; lend it on the sandbox if she has none, with a few Bardic Inspiration uses), and Tom's Tychon his Portent faces.

1. **A foe hits a friend hard.** The Drill Post hits Kartor by a lot more than a d8 could turn. Nobody is asked at the
   roll. The damage rolls, and then **Anthony sees** a popup "Cutting Words — Hearth-guard Drill Post's hit on Kartor
   Bloodaxe", "hits Kartor for N — cut the damage?", with **Cut**, **Pass**, **Not until my turn** and **Not this combat**
   (out of combat the one button **Not until the next combat**). Chat has a card "Kartor Bloodaxe is about to take N damage ·
   Lysandra Vane may cut". The damage waits.
2. **Cut.** The d8 lands in chat as Lysandra's roll, "Cutting Words — off Kartor Bloodaxe's damage"; the receipt says
   "reduced by N"; a Bardic Inspiration and her Reaction are spent; Kartor takes that much less.
3. **Not until my turn.** Lysandra's effects show "Cutting Words — muted until your next turn". The next hit on anyone asks
   her nothing. When her turn comes round the effect goes, and the next hit asks again. **Not this combat** does the same
   until the fight ends.
4. **A hit that would drop someone to 0.** Set Kartor low enough that the Drill Post's hit would down him, with Lysandra
   muted. **Anthony is asked anyway**: the popup carries **"You can keep Kartor Bloodaxe from hitting 0 Hit Points."** and
   has no mute buttons. Cut, and Kartor stands.
5. **Portent on an ordinary hit.** The Drill Post hits Kartor by a little, and Tychon holds a low face. **Tom sees**
   "Portent — Hearth-guard Drill Post's attack at Kartor Bloodaxe", "replace the 14 with the 3", Answer / Pass / the two
   mutes. Answer: a MISS, no damage — and **every screen** flashes "Tychon Thorne's Portent bends reality! Hearth-guard
   Drill Post's attack at Kartor Bloodaxe: the d20 14 becomes the 3 — 19 → 8 · MISS" for a few seconds.
6. **Portent on a friend's miss.** Kartor misses the Drill Post and Tychon holds a high face: the same popup on the miss;
   Answer: a HIT, the damage rolls, the banner.
7. **Portent on a save or a check with a DC** (sections 3–5 above): unchanged, plus the banner when Tom answers, and the
   mute buttons on his popup.
8. **What is still quiet.** Tychon's own rolls (the tick in his roll window, no banner). A bare check with no DC. A hit
   the d8 cannot turn where Lysandra already passed on the roll popup for that same hit asks her nothing more, unless it
   would down someone.

## 7. If something looks wrong

Note the time, who rolled, from where (sheet, chat card, request card), which seat saw a window, and what the card said.
The chat log keeps the rolls; the module's records sit behind the cards. Prod is not touched by any of this; the sandbox
runs a working copy and is overwritten by the next mirror from prod.
