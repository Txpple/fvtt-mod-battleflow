One dice popup, a row per feature

Empowered Spell, Healer, Elemental Adept, Piercer and Savage Attacker all do the same thing: they change dice on a roll that has already landed. Here they share one popup. The dice are chips. Each feature that fits the roll is a row, and ticking a row marks the dice it will change. Rerolls happen first; "treat as" changes apply to whatever the dice show afterward. Pick a scenario, tick and pick, then press the button.
