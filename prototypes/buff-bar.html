What it shows

The palette, unchanged

good for you — a buff you hold, a mark on a foe bad for you — a debuff on you waiting on you — Cleave armed, a hold open spent — the once-per-turn chit, the used Reaction

Nothing here is new state. Each chip is an ActiveEffect already on a sheet (flags.battleflow.mastery), its clock the platform's, its text the rule the gate already quotes.

1 · Placement

Above the hotbar reads as "mine, right now" and never covers the map. Over the token says whose it is but crowds a busy fight. The sidebar and a window survive a map scroll.

Suggested: above the hotbar, following the controlled token.

2 · Marks on others

Vexed/Sapped/Slowed live on the target's sheet but are the attacker's to spend. A second group keeps "on you" and "yours on them" from reading as one list.

Suggested: show, as their own group, named for the foe.

3 · Spent chits

"Sneak Attack — used this turn" is a record, not a buff. Struck through, it explains why the gate won't offer again; hidden, the bar is quieter.

Suggested: keep, struck through; they die with the turn anyway.

4 · Platform conditions

Prone and Restrained already have token icons. Repeating them here doubles the signal; leaving them out means the bar is only Battle Flow's chips.

Suggested: out — the token already says it.

5 · Whose bar

The controlled token's, or the player's own character regardless of selection? The GM would see whichever token is selected.

Suggested: the controlled token; a player's default control is their character.

6 · Click

First slice is a view. A click could open the effect on the sheet, or do nothing. Spending comes later, in the ghosted lane.

Suggested: click opens the effect; nothing spends from here yet.
