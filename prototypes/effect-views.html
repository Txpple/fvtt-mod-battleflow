1 · Where does the list open?

Hover and the held key read off the token and can share one renderer. The bar is a third surface with its own placement question.

Suggested: hover card + held key, no bar.

2 · Marks held on others?

Invictus's Sap sits on the Boss. Does Invictus's own list also say "you have Sapped the Boss"? Shown on the hover card as a second group; not on the bar.

Suggested: in, as a second group.

3 · Platform conditions in or out?

Prone and Poisoned already paint an icon. The list is complete with them, shorter without.

Suggested: in, tagged "no icon" only where the token cannot show it.

4 · What does a click do?

Nothing, opens the sheet's effects tab, or shows the effect's own rule text.

Suggested: the rule text, verbatim, like the popups.
