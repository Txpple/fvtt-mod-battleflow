What it costs the screen

Where does the list open?

Hover and hold-key both read off the token; the tracker and the card read off the sidebar. They are not exclusive: one renderer, several triggers.

Suggested pairing: hover card + hold-key, the same list two ways.

Whose effects, and marks on others?

Your own chips, the marks you hold on other creatures (Vex on the Boss), or both. The bar's unruled question, still open.

Shown here: own chips on every view; marks only on the turn card.

Platform conditions in or out?

Prone already paints an icon. A list that repeats it is complete; a list that omits it is a Battle Flow list.

Shown here: in, with "no icon" marked on the ones the token cannot show.

What does a click do?

Nothing, opens the sheet's effects tab, or the chip's own rule text popover.

Suggested: the rule text (law 8, verbatim), like the popups.
