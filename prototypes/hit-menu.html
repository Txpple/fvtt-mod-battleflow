The hit menu: one popup, the rows grouped by the feature that grants them

The damage offer as it opens today, with the maneuvers joining the Cunning Strike menu as a second group. Tick a row, watch the button. The rules quoted are the 2024 PHB pack's own text.

What the ruling already fixed

  1. One popup per hit. Never a menu per feature. The maneuvers are a group beside Cunning Strike, not a second window.
  2. Grouped by the feature that grants them. Combat Superiority is the group; each maneuver on the sheet is a row.
  3. Smites are out. A Bonus Action after the hit, not a rider on it.

What is read, and what is not

  1. The die comes off the pack's Superiority Die activity, scaled on the sheet (d8 at 3rd, d10 at 10th, d12 at 18th). Never typed.
  2. The pool is Combat Superiority's uses. The activity consumes one; the module never mimes it.
  3. The save is the pack's own activity on the maneuver, used at the hit target after the damage lands. DC 8 + proficiency + Str or Dex is the activity's own.
  4. The condition is whatever the activity carries: Prone, Frightened, Goaded. Pushing and Disarming carry none, so the card says it and the table does it.
  5. "Large or smaller" is a caveat line, the player's to judge, like Cunning Strike's Trip.

Rule off the prototype — RULED 2026-09-04 (user: "looks good"; the five below stand as drawn)

  1. One maneuver per attack is the 2024 text. Ticking a second maneuver drops the first. Is a maneuver and a Cunning Strike on the same hit fine? (Different features, so drawn as yes.)
  2. The die rides the damage for seven of the eight. Sweeping Attack's die goes to a second creature instead. Drawn as a row that asks for the target on the card afterwards. Slice one, or park it?
  3. Maneuvering Attack moves an ally. Drawn as a line row like Withdraw: the die rides, the card says the ally may move.
  4. The row is the name and the cost (user, 2026-09-04: "just give the cost for the sup die, just like Cunning Strike"). The save and the condition live in the rule under it and on the card after. RULED.
  5. Riposte's die already rides a riposte's damage. Should a riposte hit offer this menu as well? (Drawn as yes: a hit is a hit.)
  6. Distracting Strike gives the next ally Advantage. The pack ships it as an effect on the target; the gate can read it as an effect bend. In the slice, or later?
